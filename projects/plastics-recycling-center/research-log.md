### Research Findings

#### Finding 1: What is plastic?

**What I found:**  
Plastic is a synthetic, organic polymer made from fossil fuels, such as gas and petroleum. Over 460 million metric tons of plastic are produced every year. Plastic is used in almost all consumer and industrial activities, from construction and vehicles to electronics and agriculture.

**Why it matters to our project:**  
Since plastic is petroleum-based and found in almost every consumer item, our school will be a major source of this waste. Knowing its basic chemical structure will help with our project.

**Source:**  
[IUCN Issues Brief: Plastic Pollution](https://iucn.org/resources/issues-brief/plastic-pollution)

#### Finding 2: Problems caused by plastic waste 

Improperly discarded plastic causes severe ecosystem degradation and biodiversity loss. It breaks down into micro-plastics (<5mm) and nano-plastics (<100nm) that contaminate food, water, and human blood, while leaching carcinogenic chemicals into soil and rivers. Economically, it burdens local industries and cleanup efforts, and burning it releases greenhouse gases (CO2 and methane) that drive climate change. 

**Why it matters to our project:**  
This shows the main goal of the project. By collecting school plastic early, we stop it from breaking down into harmful micro-plastics or leaking chemicals into our local soil and water.

**Source:**  
[IUCN Issues Brief: Plastic Pollution](https://iucn.org/resources/issues-brief/plastic-pollution)

#### Finding 3: Recycling vs Reducing 

Reduction and reuse rank significantly higher than recycling in the standard waste management hierarchy. Reducing waste has a greater environmental impact because it completely eliminates the need for production, transportation, and disposal. While a small local recycling center can reduce neighborhood landfill waste and educate residents, recycling still requires energy and infrastructure, and it can create new environmental harms if the materials are heavily contaminated.  

**Why it matters to our project:**  
Since reducing waste is better than recycling, we need to teach students to use less plastic.

**Source:**  
[AIRNEX Eco Blog: Reduce vs Reuse vs Recycle](https://www.airnex.store/blogs/eco-blogs/reduce-vs-reuse-vs-recycle-which-makes-the-biggest-difference?srsltid=AfmBOorgedHwZUsYrBKgY_fo2U8MS6RxHIm7EO6GgKjFGNDEoMsQdy5R)
